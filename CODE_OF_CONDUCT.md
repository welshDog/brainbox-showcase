# Code of conduct

This is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part is expected to help keep it that way.

## Our standard

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep feedback specific and about the work, not the person.
- Respect different ways of thinking, reading and communicating. Short, plain explanations are always welcome.
- Accept a "no" or a "not now" without pressure.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Pressuring people, or posting other people's private information.
- Spam, adverts, or off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who
repeat the behaviour. To raise a concern privately, use GitHub's private reporting (the **Security** tab) and say it is a
conduct concern.

This code of conduct applies in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions.
